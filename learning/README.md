# Learning route

This route connects the thermodynamics of individual state transformations to work extraction from an unknown source with unbounded energy levels. It follows one educational anchor:

**Matteo Lostaglio, *An introductory review of the resource theory approach to thermodynamics*, arXiv:1807.11549v2, 7 October 2019.** Published in *Reports on Progress in Physics* **82**, 114001 (2019). [Versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.11549v2) · [Versioned HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1807.11549v2) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6633/ab46e5).

## Start with the physical task

The [anchor bridge](anchor_bridge.md) explains the source, allowed operations, and battery; works through an exact energy-transfer example; and connects the review to the universal protocol. It translates the work bit into a fixed oscillator and explains why collective coherence, battery entropy, and the order of limits matter.

The [30-milestone map](milestones.md) organizes the project's understanding targets into two phases:

| Phase | Milestones | Scientific role |
|---|---|---|
| Background and preliminaries | M01–M14 | Build states, entropy, thermal operations, collective statistics, and a complete finite-dimensional extraction engine. |
| Narrative and innovation | M15–M30 | Extend the engine through thermal cutoffs and universal control, then establish its rates, examples, converse, and exact-output boundary. |

The allocation puts the physical and statistical ingredients before the infinite-dimensional construction. The final milestones join the separate output guarantees into the complete theorem.
